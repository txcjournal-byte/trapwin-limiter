TrapWin Limiter

TW-2 clipper & true-peak limiter for trap and underground music
USER MANUAL · VERSION 1.0.0 · WINDOWS & MACOS · VST3 / AU

1. What it is

TrapWin Limiter makes your beats loud without losing the punch of the kick and the weight of the 808. It combines four stages in one plugin, in this order:

  1. Input gain, punch and stereo width: how hard you drive the processing.
  2. 808 enhancer and saturation: harmonics that make sub bass audible on phones and laptop speakers, plus warmth and density.
  3. Clipper (Tape, Hard or Soft): shaves off the sharpest peaks before the limiter, which keeps drums punchy at high loudness.
  4. True-peak limiter with lookahead: holds the output safely under your ceiling, including the inter-sample peaks that cause distortion after MP3/AAC conversion on streaming services.

Built-in LUFS and true-peak metering, a streaming loudness preview and a "delta" listen mode help you decide how loud is loud enough.

2. Installation

Windows

  1. Close your DAW.
  2. Run TrapWin_Limiter_v1.0.0_Windows_Setup.exe and follow the steps. It installs the VST3 into the standard plugin folder.
  3. Manual install instead: copy the folder Manual Install\TrapLimit.vst3 into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\.
  4. Start your DAW and rescan plugins (FL Studio: Options › Manage plugins › Find installed plugins).

macOS

  1. Close your DAW.
  2. Double-click TrapWin_Limiter_v1.0.0_macOS.pkg and follow the installer. Choose which formats to install: VST3 and/or Audio Unit (AU, needed for Logic Pro and GarageBand).
  3. Manual install instead: from the Manual Install folder copy TrapLimit.vst3 to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ and TrapLimit.component to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/.
  4. Start your DAW. Logic Pro scans new Audio Units automatically; in other hosts rescan plugins.

Load TrapWin Limiter (vendor TrapWin) as the last plugin on your master or on a bus.

Requirements: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) or macOS 10.15 or newer (Intel or Apple Silicon, native); a 64-bit VST3 or AU host; OpenGL graphics. Sample rates from 44.1 kHz to 192 kHz are supported.

3. Quick start: a loud trap master in one minute

  1. Put TrapWin Limiter last on the master channel.
  2. Choose the preset Trap Master Loud (click the preset name, or use the < > arrows).
  3. Play the loudest part of your beat and turn INPUT up until the needle shows about 3-6 dB of gain reduction on the loudest hits.
  4. Leave CEILING at −1.0 dB for streaming releases (−0.3 dB is common for club or beat-store files).
  5. Press A to switch to setting B, set INPUT lower, and compare both. In the settings page, turn on 0 dB MATCH for a fair comparison at equal level.
TIP
If the 808 starts to "fart" or the kick loses its click, lower INPUT a little or switch the clipper from HARD to TAPE or SOFT. Loudness that ruins the groove is never worth it.

Meter (centre)

The needle shows the total gain reduction of the clipper and limiter together (scale 0 to 20 dB). The left display shows integrated loudness in LUFS, the right display the highest true peak in dBTP; it turns red if the output ever exceeded the ceiling. Click inside the meter window to reset the measurements, for example before playing the song from the start.

5. Settings page (MORE)

Click a box to switch it; drag up or down (or use the mouse wheel) on value boxes. Double-click a value to reset it. Close the page with X.

SETTINGRANGE · DEFAULTWHAT IT DOES
LOOKAHEAD0.1-5 ms · 1.5How far ahead the limiter looks to catch peaks. Longer = cleaner on fast transients. Overall plugin latency stays constant.
ATTACK0.05-5 ms · 0.5How smoothly gain reduction starts. Very short attack is tight but can distort low bass.
STEREO LINK0-100 % · 100100 % = both channels get the same gain reduction (stable stereo image). Lower values limit each side more independently.
WIDTH0-200 % · 100Stereo width before processing. 0 % = mono, 200 % = extra wide.
PUNCH0-100 % · 0Transient shaper before the clipper. Brings out the attack of drums. The PUNCH mode adds to this value.
AUTO RELEASEon / off · onAdapts the release to the music: fast on short peaks, slower on sustained loud parts. Reduces pumping.
LIMITERon / off · onTurns the limiter stage off, leaving only gain, saturation and clipper. Use it to use the plugin as a pure clipper. With the limiter off, the output is no longer held under the ceiling.
OVERSAMPLE1x / 2x / 4x · 2xRuns the clipper and saturation at a higher sample rate to reduce aliasing (harshness). 4x sounds cleanest and uses the most CPU.
SUB SPLITon / off · offWith the clipper on, the sub band (below 120 Hz) gets its own gentle clipping and is not hit by the main clipper. Keeps the 808 round while drums are clipped hard.
HPF SIDECHAINon / off · offThe limiter recovers faster when gain reduction is caused by sub bass. Less pumping on heavy 808s.
MID / SIDEon / off · offLimits the mid and side signals separately; the side recovers faster to keep the mix wide.
DITHER 16 BITon / off · offAdds low-level dither. Turn on only when you export a 16-bit WAV directly from this plugin.
STREAM PREVIEWOff / −14 / −16 / −9 LUFSTurns the output down the way streaming services would (−14 LUFS Spotify/YouTube style, −16 Apple Music style, −9 loud reference). Use it to check if pushing harder still makes sense. Turn it off before exporting.
0 dB MATCHon / off · offCompensates INPUT and DRIVE gain on the output, so you hear the processing at roughly the original level instead of just "louder".
DELTAon / off · offPlays only what the clipper and limiter remove. If you can clearly hear your kick or vocal in delta mode, you are pushing too hard.

7. Tips

How loud?

Streaming services turn loud songs down to about −14 LUFS. A master at −7 LUFS is not louder there; it only has less punch. Use STREAM PREVIEW to hear this. For trap, −9 to −6 LUFS integrated is common; let your ears decide.

Clipper first, limiter second

Let the clipper take the first 1-3 dB of the sharpest peaks (fast drum transients) and the limiter the rest. This sounds louder and punchier than limiting alone.

Keep the 808 clean

Turn on MONO SUB and HPF SIDECHAIN, and try SUB SPLIT. Use 808 BOOST for presence on small speakers instead of more low-end EQ.

Check with DELTA

Short clicks in delta mode are fine. If you can hear the melody or vocal, back off INPUT or DRIVE.

9. Troubleshooting

Plugin not foundWindows: check that the folder is C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\TrapLimit.vst3. macOS: plugins are in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 and /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components. Then rescan plugins in your DAW. In FL Studio, enable "Rescan previously verified plugins".
Plugin listed twiceAn older copy is installed in another folder. Delete the old one and rescan.
Window too big or smallDrag the window corner to resize. The size adapts to your Windows display scaling.
Output quieter than expectedSTREAM PREVIEW or 0 dB MATCH is on (settings page). Turn them off before exporting.
No soundCheck DELTA is off and the plugin is not bypassed.
